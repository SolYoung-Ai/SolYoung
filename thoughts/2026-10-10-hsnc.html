即使没人看见，我也愿意做。因为创作本身，就让我感到充实。

我一直很害怕一件事情。有一天突然回头，发现自己这一年和上一年没有什么区别。做着一样的工作，过着一样的生活，认识一样的人，甚至连自己想要什么，都已经懒得再想。我好像一直在逃离这种状态。小时候，我喜欢拆零件、画画、写歌词和复杂的东西。那个时候没有人告诉我应该怎么做。我只是觉得好玩。一个东西原本是什么样子并不重要。重要的是，我能不能按照自己的想法，把它变成另外一个样子。

后来我长大了。我依然喜欢做同样的事情。只不过手里的东西，从零件和树枝，变成了相机、电脑，再到现在的AI和各种数字工具。工具一直在变。但我喜欢的事情好像一直没有变：把一个原本不存在的东西，变成真实存在的东西。

我很早就离开了学校。后来一个人去了深圳。那时候其实没有什么宏大的目标。我只是很想知道：这个世界到底是什么样子。所以我去了一个完全陌生的城市。开始工作，开始赚钱，也开始自己摸索。后来我接触到短视频。我开始做自己的账号。当我第一次发现，自己做出来的东西真的有人看、有人关注，甚至能够凭借这些东西找到工作的时候，我第一次意识到：原来一个人的想法和能力，真的可以变成一种价值。

后来我去了广州。工作越来越成熟，平台越来越大，流程越来越完整。我也确实在里面做出了成绩。但与此同时，我开始越来越不舒服。会议变多了。规则变多了。需要配合的人变多了。每天需要完成的事情越来越明确。我开始觉得自己像一个越来越精密的齿轮。我可以把事情做好。甚至可以把事情做得很好。但我越来越想知道：如果让我自己决定，我到底能做出什么？

所以后来我离开了。我也尝试过很多东西。自媒体、带货、私域、服装等等……有些赚到过钱，有些没有。有些做了一段时间以后，我发现自己并不喜欢。于是就放下。这几年下来，我越来越明白一件事情：不是所有能够赚钱的事情，都值得我一直做。我真正想找的，是一个我愿意长期钻进去的领域。一个可以让我不断学习、不断创造、不断变强的地方。

所以我来了杭州。换一个城市，换一种生活。开始拍更多自己真正喜欢的东西。我有一个叫@楊十八呀的帐号，是我记录生活和影像的地方，还有一个叫@野心互联工作室，是我在24年底做个人成长及健康类的内容频道，现在我还经营一个新的帐号叫@SolYoung，是我记录创作的帐号。

最近我发现，AI给了我一种以前没有过的感觉：很多以前只是“想想而已”的东西，现在真的可以被做出来。一个网站。一个工具。一段视频。一个新的视觉。甚至一个以前从来没有存在过的东西。只要我愿意花时间，它就有可能真的出现。这对我来说很有吸引力。

所以现在的我，越来越不想用一个职业去定义自己。摄影师？剪辑师？内容创作者？AI创作者？产品设计者？这些都只是我现在正在使用的工具。我更愿意把自己理解成一个：把想法变成现实的人。我想到一个东西，就想试试。发现一个工具不好用，就想改。觉得一个网站应该是另外一种样子，就自己做。想拍一个东西就拿起相机。想做一个账号，就开始做。想研究一个新的领域，就进去看看。我喜欢的并不是“做成一个产品”这件事情本身。我喜欢的是那个过程：一个想法出现。然后一点一点把它变成现实。最后真的有一个东西存在于这个世界上。这种从零到有的感觉，让我觉得很有意思。

我现在依然没有完全确定自己最后会走到哪里。但我越来越确定自己不想回到哪里。我不想过一种可以提前看到下一年的生活。如果今天的我和一年前的我没有区别，我会觉得很可怕。所以我愿意换城市。愿意换工作。愿意重新学习。愿意尝试新的东西。愿意做一些可能失败的事情。甚至愿意推翻自己过去做过的决定。因为我觉得，人生本来就应该不断更新。就像一个游戏。你不断获得新的装备、新的技能、新的地图。然后重新开始下一关。

我给自己的网站设置了一个100小时的变化周期。每过100个小时，我都允许它发生一点变化。可以增加新的东西。可以删掉旧的东西。也可以推翻以前的想法。但有一件事情不会变：继续往前。

我觉得这其实也是我想要的人生。不是一定要变得多么成功。而是希望几年以后回头看，能够发现：我真的一直在变化。一直在学习。一直在创造。一直在靠近一个更好的自己。

有人可能会问：如果有一天，你已经有足够多的钱，不需要为了生存工作了，你还会继续做这些事情吗？我的答案是：会。
甚至可能会做得更认真。因为钱对我来说，更像是一种自由。它可以让我少一些顾虑，多一些勇气。但它不是我真正想要的东西。我真正喜欢的，是创造。我喜欢研究一个东西。喜欢把它做出来。喜欢看着一个原本不存在的东西慢慢出现。也希望有一天，它能够被别人看见。

当然，如果没有人看见，我还是会继续做。因为创作本身，就已经让我觉得充实。但我也希望它能够被看见。因为被理解，也会带来力量。我相信真正优秀的东西，最终会被别人看到。所以我现在能做的事情其实很简单：
继续创造。
继续学习。
继续尝试。

我不知道以后会成为一个什么样的人。也不知道最后会走到哪里。但我知道，我不想让自己停在原地。我想一直保持好奇。一直去看看新的世界。一直在一个真正喜欢的领域里往下钻。一直把自己觉得有意思的事情做出来。然后不断地更新自己。

如果人生真的像一场游戏，那我希望自己不是在重复刷同一个关卡。而是在不断解锁新的地图。至于最后能走到哪里。我不知道。但我很期待。因为直到现在，我还是很想知道这个世界到底会变成什么样子。

I've always feared one thing: suddenly looking back and finding this year no different from the last — same work, same life, same people, too lazy to even think about what I actually want. I've been running from that state my whole life. As a kid I loved taking things apart, drawing, writing lyrics, and messing with complex things. Back then no one told me how to do it. I just thought it was fun. What a thing was originally didn't matter. What mattered was whether I could reshape it into something else, according to my own ideas.

Then I grew up. I still loved doing the same things. Only the objects in my hands changed — from parts and branches to cameras and computers, and now to AI and all kinds of digital tools. The tools keep changing. But what I loved never really changed: turning something that didn't exist into something real.

I left school early. Later I went to Shenzhen on my own. I didn't have some grand goal then. I just wanted to know: what does the world actually look like? So I went to a completely unfamiliar city. Started working, started earning, started figuring things out on my own. Then I got into short video. I started my own accounts. When I first found that things I made actually got watched, followed, and even helped me land a job, I realized for the first time: a person's ideas and ability can really turn into value.

Later I went to Guangzhou. Work got more mature, platforms got bigger, processes got more complete. I did achieve results there. But at the same time, I started to feel more and more uneasy. More meetings. More rules. More people to coordinate with. More clearly defined tasks every day. I started to feel like an increasingly precise gear. I could get things done. Even do them well. But I kept wanting to know: if I decided for myself, what could I actually make?

So later I left. I tried a lot of things. Self-media, e-commerce, private traffic, clothing, and so on… Some made money, some didn't. Some I did for a while then found I didn't like, so I let go. Over these years I've come to understand one thing: not everything that makes money is worth doing forever. What I'm really looking for is a field I'm willing to dive into long-term — a place where I can keep learning, keep creating, keep getting better.

So I came to Hangzhou. A new city, a new life. Started filming more of the things I truly love. I have an account called @楊十八呀, where I record life and visuals; another called @野心互联工作室, a personal-growth and health content channel I started at the end of 2024; and now a new account called @SolYoung, where I record my creative work.

Lately I've found AI gives me a feeling I never had before: so many things that used to be just “something I'd think about” can now actually be made. A website. A tool. A video. A new visual. Even something that never existed before. As long as I'm willing to spend the time, it can actually show up. That really attracts me.

So now, increasingly, I don't want to define myself by one job title. Photographer? Editor? Content creator? AI creator? Product designer? These are just the tools I happen to be using right now. I'd rather understand myself as: someone who turns ideas into reality. When I think of something, I want to try it. When a tool isn't good enough, I want to fix it. When I feel a website should look different, I build it myself. Want to shoot something, pick up the camera. Want to start an account, just start. Want to explore a new field, go in and look. What I love isn't the act of “shipping a product” itself. I love the process: an idea appears, then I turn it into reality bit by bit, until something actually exists in the world. That feeling of going from zero to something is what I find interesting.

I still don't fully know where I'll end up. But I'm more and more sure of where I don't want to go. I don't want a life whose next year I can already see in advance. If today's me is no different from a year ago, I'd find that terrifying. So I'm willing to change cities. Willing to change jobs. Willing to relearn. Willing to try new things. Willing to do things that might fail. Even willing to overturn decisions I made before. Because I feel life itself should be constantly updating. Like a game: you keep getting new gear, new skills, new maps, then start the next level.

I set my website a 100-hour cycle of change. Every 100 hours, I allow it to change a little. Add new things. Delete old things. Even overturn past ideas. But one thing won't change: keep moving forward.

I think this is the kind of life I want, too. Not necessarily to become wildly successful. But to look back in a few years and find: I really have been changing. Learning. Creating. Getting closer to a better version of myself.

Someone might ask: if one day you had enough money and didn't need to work to survive, would you still keep doing these things? My answer: yes.
I might even do them more seriously. Because money to me is more like a kind of freedom. It lets me worry less, be braver. But it's not what I truly want. What I love is creating. I love studying something, building it, watching something that didn't exist slowly appear, and hoping one day it'll be seen.

Of course, if no one sees it, I'll still keep doing it. Because creating itself already feels fulfilling. But I also hope it gets seen. Because being understood also gives strength. I believe truly good things will eventually be seen. So what I can do now is simple:
Keep creating.
Keep learning.
Keep trying.

I don't know who I'll become. Or where I'll end up. But I know I don't want to stay still. I want to stay curious. Keep seeing new worlds. Keep diving deeper into a field I truly love. Keep making the things I find interesting. And keep updating myself.

If life really is a game, I hope I'm not just replaying the same level — I'm unlocking new maps. Where I end up, I don't know. But I'm looking forward to it. Because even now, I still want to see what the world will become.
